# Project Nexus
Nexus is the codename for a new, R&D based, and probably inhumane experiment where I am singlehandedly attempting to resurrect a deceased fly.

Google (see research papers below) has partnered with HHMI Janelia to map an entire fly brain and publish the schematics on the internet for all to use. Unfortunately, our fly friend is dead. I'm taking it upon myself to develop an algorithm and cord to allow a fly to live and develop in a virtual environment, specifically a Unity 6000f1 game. 

The environment will compose of the following, where the fly will undergo hundreds of tests:
- Obstacle Course: The fly must be able to navigate through holes, weave around pillars, and reach an end platform with full stability.
- Feeding Area: I will observe how the fly eats and digests its day-to-day nutrients in a real-world environment.
- Mating: I will attempt to get two different fly neural networks running, male and female, and force-breed to see the outcome.
- Survival: I will measure how long the fly lives in a 3-month tracking period. If the fly surpasses the 3 months, the experiment will continue.

While this may seem inhumane, at least we aren't testing on humans... yet. With the approval of the Federal Government, Project Nexus would like to test on a donated brain from a deceased individual for trails within a simulated environment, testing life from 0-100. The resources needed will be up towards $14,620,000 USD.
To learn more, go to https://research.wsgpolar.me to learn how I'm doing what I'm doing, and why. It also includes reports and experiment results. Nexus will not put the fly under extreme measures (torture) or any inhumane acts.
